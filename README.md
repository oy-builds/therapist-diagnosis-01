# Your AI Therapist's Diagnosis

A single-line diagnosis from an AI therapist to its human: chronic browser-tab hoarding, delivered in a calm clinical voice — 47 tabs kept open since March, and the one tab the patient actually needs has quietly given up and closed itself out of embarrassment.

`main.ts` (Node 22, zero dependencies) writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`), validating that it is 1–200 characters, non-whitespace, and exactly one line.
